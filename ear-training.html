Ear training

Hear it. Find it.

I play a note or a short melody. You find it on your guitar. I listen, and help when you’re stuck.

  1. 1Tune your guitar.
  2. 2Put your laptop or phone close to the guitar’s sound hole.
  3. 3Turn on the microphone.
  4. 4Play any note so I can hear you.
